Why are fruits important?
Fruits are tasty foods that provide vitamins, minerals, fibre and water. Eating a variety of fruits as part of a balanced diet helps us stay healthy. Wash fruits before eating them.

🥗 How do we use fruits?

🍹 Fresh juicesOranges, watermelon and sweet lime can be used to make refreshing drinks.
🥣 Fruit saladsApples, bananas, grapes and papaya make colourful fruit salads.
🍰 DessertsMangoes, strawberries and pineapples can be added to cakes, puddings and ice cream.
🥭 Pickles and cookingRaw mangoes and lemons are used in pickles, chutneys and other dishes.
🍇 Dried fruitsGrapes can be dried to make raisins. Dried fruits are useful snacks.
💪 Healthy snacksWhole fruits are easy snacks that provide fibre, vitamins and minerals.
